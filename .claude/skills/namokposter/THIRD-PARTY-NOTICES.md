The OFL and public-domain faces are whole fonts: the publisher's own WOFF2 where there is one, else the
publisher's TrueType or OpenType file converted to WOFF2 with Google's woff2 encoder and nothing else
changed (no subsetting, no renaming). The four faces under their publisher's own terms are the publisher's
OpenType files, not converted and not changed, beside the publisher's licence. What those terms forbid:

- 여기어때 잘난체: selling the font files; redistributing a modified or reworked font; registering a trademark on a CI/BI set in it.
- 에스코어 드림: changing or selling the font files.
- 이사만루: selling the font files, or a modified version.
- 넥슨 메이플스토리: redistributing a modified or edited font file; selling the font.

Each also says that images of printed matter and advertising set in the face may be used in the
publisher's promotion (anyone who does not want that may ask the publisher), and recommends naming the
face as the source. None of this restricts a poster set in these faces: posters may be used freely,
commercially included.

The four NAVER families are not on Fontsource. Their licence is NAVER's notice for the Nanum fonts
(https://help.naver.com/service/30016/contents/18088, 네이버 나눔글꼴 라이선스안내), which names
NanumSquareRound, NanumBarunPen, MaruBuri and NanumSquareNeo under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the
files are NAVER's own web-font files, not subset and not converted (the names are Reserved Font Names).
A name ending in " KR" in the font sheets (`"Abril Fatface KR"`, `"Playfair Display KR"`) is a CSS
alias of the same unmodified files drawn 6% larger beside Hangul, not another font.

`assets/fonts-extra/` is an optional display-font pack (about 62 MB; `fonts.css`, `fonts-kr2.css`,
`fonts-kr3.css`, `fonts-kr4.css` and `fonts-latin2.css` declare its faces). Most type themes beyond the first eight
(`bold`, `friendly`, `impact`, `swiss`, `elegant`, `editorial`, `brutal`, `outline`) use it and fall
back to the core fonts in `assets/fonts/` when the folder is absent, so it can be left out of a
size-limited package.

Font files other than Pretendard, Galmuri11, the four NAVER families and type pack 4 come from the
Fontsource packages (unicode-range subsets, woff2). Each font folder contains its licence text. Posters made
with these fonts may be used freely, including commercially; the fonts themselves may not be sold on
their own. Why each face is in the bundle — its licence and how widely it is used — is set out in
`references/fonts.md`.

Faces fetched at the user's request by `scripts/fonts.mjs add` are not part of this package: they are
kept in the user's cache folder with their own licence text, and named in each poster's `report.json`.

Blender and Playwright/Chromium are not bundled; they are used if installed on the machine.
